## Reading these numbers honestly
- **Layer 2 is a sanity result on both splits:** the clips are the library scam scripts read by other TTS voices. The honest Layer 2 test needs the holdout paraphrases and the human recordings (docs/06), which do not exist yet.
- **Hinglish is the weak spot.** On the test split every missed scam but one and all 6 false alarms are Hinglish clips read by the British voice (enGB_Ryan): Whisper (forced to English output) turns that accent and language mix into near-nonsense, so neither the script layer nor the lexicon has evidence (Hinglish AUC 0.745 vs 1.0 for English).
- **Live words-only detection is slow on test** (median 8.2 s after the first red-flag phrase; none within the 5 s target): the score needs more sentences before the script similarity crosses the threshold. Fingerprint replays alert ~2 s into the call.
- The fingerprint "near-copy" rate counts the library voice at the other speaking rate matching the *correct* script; it is reported separately from false matches (legit audio or the wrong script), which were 0 on both splits.
- Thresholds were not changed after seeing the test results.
