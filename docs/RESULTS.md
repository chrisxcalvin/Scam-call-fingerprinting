## Reading these numbers honestly
- **Dev and test use the library scripts** (read by other TTS voices). They show that exact audio and near-verbatim scripts are caught, not that new scams are.
- **The holdout is the generalisation test.** On it, recall is 11%: reworded scams score about 0.62 similarity to the library (below the 0.633 floor 55% of the time) and often contain none of the lexicon's keywords. False alarms stay at 0%.
- **Hinglish is weakest** (holdout script AUC 0.63; test 0.75), and British-accented Hinglish defeats Whisper.
- **Live detection** (test split): fingerprint replays alert about 2 s into the call; for new voices the median alert comes 8.2 s after the first red-flag phrase. On the holdout only 8% of scam streams alert at all.
- The fingerprint "near-copy" rate counts the library voice at another speaking rate matching the *correct* script; it is reported separately from false matches (legit audio or the wrong script), which were 0 on all three splits.
- Thresholds were not changed after seeing the test or holdout results.
