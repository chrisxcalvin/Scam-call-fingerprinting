## Reading these numbers honestly
- **Test 2 is the cleanest result:** generated by a different model and prompt than the training data, SHA-256 recorded before training, run once with settings frozen on dev 2. It is TTS audio only, so real human speech is untested.
- **The holdout is a "second look":** its diagnosis was seen while the model was being designed, so it is not blind any more.
- **Upload vs live:** upload analysis sees the whole call; live analysis acts on partial transcripts and the risk level only rises, so early look-alike openings cause false alerts (test 2: about a third of legit streams, holdout: about a fifth). Fixing this (for example requiring the evidence to persist over several ticks, or letting contradicting evidence lower the level) is the main open improvement; it must be designed on dev 2 and then re-tested on fresh data.
- **Weakest scam type:** KYC/OTP (12 of 40 test-2 clips missed); scam-type identification is 75% on test 2.
- About two thirds of legit test-2 calls fall in the amber "be careful" band (30-60): not flagged, but noisy.
- Fingerprint results are unchanged by the new model: exact recordings are caught, zero false matches on every split.
