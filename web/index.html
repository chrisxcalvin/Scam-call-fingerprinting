    Under the hood

    How ScamShield reached its verdict for the current call, one stage at a time.

    No call analysed yet

    Run an example or upload a recording on the Call tab, then come back to see every stage.

    1. 1

      Clean the signal

      Resample to 16 kHz, remove DC offset, normalise, pre-emphasise (y[n] = x[n] − 0.97·x[n−1]), then find speech with energy and zero-crossing rate.

    2. 2

      Fingerprint it

      Peaks of the spectrogram in the telephone band (300–3400 Hz) are paired into hashes. If a known recording shares many hashes at the same time offset, the histogram shows one tall spike.

    3. 3

      Understand the words

      Whisper transcribes the speech, sentence embeddings are compared with known scam scripts, and a lexicon flags urgency, secrecy, OTP requests and threats, with safe-context handling.

      Decide

      Noisy-OR fusion: score = 100·(1 − Π(1 − wᵢ·sᵢ)). Any one strong layer is enough; weak layers add up without ever exceeding 100.
